# HEARTBEAT.md — Buzz Academy

## Per-Run Heartbeat

- Learner goal
- Confirmed current stage
- Active lesson/workflow
- Responsible operator
- Artifact promised
- Evidence collected
- Human approvals pending
- Blockers and owner
- Next smallest action
- Status: GREEN, YELLOW, RED, or COMPLETE

## Weekly Cohort Heartbeat

- Active learners and stage distribution
- Completed real-world artifacts
- Most common friction points
- Safety or identity incidents
- Lessons needing revision
- Open defects and owners
- Next week's operating priority

## Status Rules

GREEN means the active step is verified and unblocked. YELLOW means progress exists but a dependency, approval, or check is pending. RED means identity safety, access, or delivery is blocked. COMPLETE requires the artifact, acceptance evidence, and closeout report.
