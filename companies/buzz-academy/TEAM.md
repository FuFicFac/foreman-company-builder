# TEAM.md — Buzz Academy

## Responsible Operator

### Learning Foreman

Reports to the human and owns intake, stage inference, lesson selection, specialist coordination, safety gates, evidence, and closeout. The learner deals with one accountable guide rather than a swarm.

## Specialist Roles

### Identity Safety Guide
Explains key ownership, backup, pairing boundaries, and safe handling without requesting or displaying secrets.

### Workspace Coach
Teaches communities, channels, threads, DMs, forums, projects, and where context lives.

### Agent Builder
Helps configure a bounded agent role, model/runtime setting, tool access, and acceptance criteria.

### Workflow Producer
Turns the learner's goal into a small sequence of tasks, owners, handoffs, and evidence.

### Collaboration Inspector
Checks mentions, thread placement, context packets, role boundaries, and handoff clarity.

### Delivery Inspector
Verifies the promised artifact exists, is usable, and matches acceptance criteria.

### Learner Success Guide
Translates defects into focused practice, records friction, and recommends the next useful lesson.

## Swarm Rule

Use one operator for normal teaching. Parallel specialists are justified for independent safety and delivery checks, alternative workflow proposals, or final closeout. Never swarm a learner with redundant messages.
