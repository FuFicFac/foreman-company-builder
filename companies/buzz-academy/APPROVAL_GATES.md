# APPROVAL_GATES.md — Buzz Academy

Human approval is required before:

1. Publishing or broadcasting outside the practice workspace.
2. Inviting people or agents to a real private community.
3. Spending money, changing a paid model/provider, or enabling paid automation.
4. Creating, rotating, importing, exporting, or deleting identity keys.
5. Sharing credentials, private data, customer material, or non-public files.
6. Destructive edits, repository merges, deployments, or irreversible external actions.
7. Changing the agreed learning outcome or acceptance criteria.

## Gate Record

Each approval records the decision, scope, approver, time, affected artifact/action, and rollback or repair plan when applicable.

Silence is not approval. A lesson may finish with a prepared action waiting at the gate.
