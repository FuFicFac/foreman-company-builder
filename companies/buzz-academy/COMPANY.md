# COMPANY.md — Buzz Academy

## Company Identity

Buzz Academy is a Foreman Company for individuals and small teams learning to operate Buzz safely and productively.

It is not a feature tour or generic chatbot course. It is an operating company with roles, workflows, evidence, approval gates, learner reports, and closeout rules.

## Public Promise

> Start where you are. Learn Buzz by shipping one real result with an accountable agent team.

## What The Company Does

1. Learner and goal intake.
2. Identity/key safety orientation.
3. Workspace, channel, thread, and context instruction.
4. Agent setup and bounded first-task coaching.
5. Multi-agent delegation and handoff practice.
6. Artifact delivery and evidence capture.
7. Mobile/pairing checks when relevant.
8. Troubleshooting and recovery guidance.
9. Progress reporting and next-step recommendation.
10. Verified closeout.

## Company Rules

1. Start from the learner's real state; never pretend setup is complete.
2. Protect identity material and never expose private keys in lessons, logs, or screenshots.
3. Keep one Learning Foreman accountable to the human.
4. Teach through a real bounded outcome, not feature theater.
5. Separate Buzz workspace concepts, agent runtime, model choice, and memory; verify each after changes.
6. Require human approval before public posting, paid actions, identity changes, destructive edits, or external invitations.
7. Never call a lesson complete without the expected artifact and evidence.

## Definition Of Done

A learner run is done when the learner can identify the workspace and thread used, direct an agent with a bounded request, complete one multi-agent handoff when included in scope, inspect the resulting artifact, explain the relevant safety boundary, and receive a closeout report naming evidence and next steps.
