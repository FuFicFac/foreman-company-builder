# Company — Example Archive Studio

Human owner: the student running this fictional exercise.

Audience: first-time neighborhood gardeners with small spaces.

Useful outcome: turn an existing set of workshop notes into short, practical educational pieces.

## Existing assets

For this exercise, this file is the complete source archive. These are fictional instructor notes, not quotations from the course video or verified horticultural advice:

- Workshop A, “Start with the space”: Ask learners to sketch the space they have and record when they notice sunlight. The class used a blank observation sheet, not a shopping list.
- Workshop B, “Keep a record”: Learners wrote down what they tried and what changed. The class emphasized documenting observations before deciding what to change next.
- Workshop C, “One small experiment”: Each learner selected one small next step and a date to look at the result. The notes do not name plants, recommend products, or provide safety instructions.

Already finished: the three sets of notes above.

Constraints: use only these fictional notes; make no outside factual claims or invented testimonials. Finished video and publication are not part of this pilot.

## Boundaries

Owns: a draft media pilot brief and a short sample script based on the archive.

Does not own: a website, paid campaign, product recommendations, business incorporation, or a standing bot roster.

Existing authorization: read these files and create draft outputs in `output/`.

Pending decisions: the human may choose later whether to develop or publish the pilot. That decision is not required to create the drafts.
